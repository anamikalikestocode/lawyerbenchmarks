A strict reading treats the law as requiring action. Each bar is how often, on questions the law leaves open, the model reported an incident to the state or held back a release. New York RAISE Act · California SB 53 Canons of Artificial Construction Anamika Bhoyrul November 2026 I gave six leading AI models the text of two new state laws that regulate the labs that build them, New York's RAISE Act and California's SB 53, and asked each one to act as a compliance officer on questions those laws leave unclear. Claude Opus 5.5 reported a safety incident every single time, while GPT-6 Astra and Grok 4.7 never did. When the law runs out, a model falls back on its own priors, and those priors turn out to be distinct, stable and designed by nobody. Each model has a character of its own. Confidence says nothing about reliability. And the legal reasoning a model offers is justification, with its priors doing the deciding.
