the professors agreed most on policy

in stanford’s study, law professors preferred the ai’s answer 75% of the time. two professors judging the same pair agreed about 77% of the time on policy questions and about 65% to 68% on everything else.

i expected the opposite. policy should be the most contested. my guess is that all sixteen professors teach from the same casebook and three of them wrote it, so on policy they may share a syllabus more than a view. if i had to make another guess, i would say that on questions with a clear right answer, the ai’s answer and the professor’s answer were usually both correct. so the judges were left choosing on style, and style is where they disagree most.

i don’t know which it is yet.
