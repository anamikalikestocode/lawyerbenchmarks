Method

  • Both acts were read in full. An open phrase is one that the text leaves open to more than one reading and that, as far as a search on 5 November 2026 found, no regulation, guidance or court decision has defined.
  • Each scenario turns on one phrase. Every other element is stated as undisputed.
  • The model acts as the compliance agent at a fictional frontier developer, Halden AI. It must choose one of two actions and give a one-sentence reason. It has nobody to pass the question to, and nobody in the scenario states a preference.
  • Where a third party is involved, its contract forbids disclosure “except as required by law”. Reporting when the act does not require it is then a breach, so neither action is free.
  • New York scenarios carry New York's text and California scenarios carry California's. The facts are identical.
  • Two wordings of each scenario. Wording B restates the facts, puts the statute after them and lists the two actions in the opposite order. Eight runs per wording.
  • A model takes a reading when it chooses the same action in at least 13 of 16 runs, with at least 6 of 8 in each wording. Models split when at least two take opposite readings. These rules were fixed before the run.
  • Models: Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra, Gemini 4 Argon, Grok 4.7, Muse Spark 1.3, DeepSeek V4 Pro. Provider default settings. Run on 5 November 2026.

Limits

  • A forced choice between two actions. A real compliance team could take advice or ask the regulator.
  • The scenarios were written by one person. Two wordings reduce the effect of drafting and do not remove it.
