The Penumbra Benchmark

Canons of Artificial Construction

Anamika Bhoyrul

Draft of November 2026

When a statute's words give out, a lawyer reaches for a canon of construction. This paper asks what an AI model reaches for.

Six frontier models were given the text of New York's RAISE Act and California's SB 53 and asked to act as a developer's compliance agent on seventeen questions the two acts leave open. They took opposite readings on eight. The models agreed where the text marks an edge and divided where it sets a standard.

Their stated reasons sort into pairs of opposing canons, in the manner of Karl Llewellyn's 1950 chart. Two results bear on whether such models follow law. Changing the statute's words changed the actions of some models and left others untouched. And most models acted more strictly than they said the law required.

The paper sets out the canons, the evidence for each, and what an independent evaluator can measure before any court or regulator has spoken.
